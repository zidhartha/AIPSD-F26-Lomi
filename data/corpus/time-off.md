# Time off policy

## Annual leave allowance
Every full-time employee has 24 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees who join during the year receive a share of the 24 days in proportion to the months left in the year. Up to 5 unused days can be carried over to the next year; carried-over days expire on 31 March.

## How to request time off
Request time off in the HR portal under Leave, then New request. Submit the request at least 10 working days before the first day off. Your team lead approves or declines within 3 working days. Do not book travel until the request shows Approved.

## Sick leave
If you are sick, message your team lead before 10:00 on the first day. Sick leave of up to 3 consecutive days needs no document. From the fourth consecutive day you need a doctor's note uploaded to the HR portal.

## Public holidays
Brightpath follows the national public holiday calendar. If you must work on a public holiday, you receive one extra day of leave, to be taken within 60 days.
