# Requesting time off

To request paid time off (PTO), submit a request in the HR portal at least two weeks before your first day away. Your manager approves or declines the request within three working days.

Full-time employees accrue 20 paid vacation days per year, plus public holidays. Unused days roll over once, up to five days, into the next year.

Sick leave is separate from vacation. For sick leave, notify your manager by email on the first morning; no portal request is needed for an absence shorter than three days.
