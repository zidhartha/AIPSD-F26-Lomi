# Remote and hybrid work

## Office days
The team works from the office on Tuesday and Thursday. Other days you may work from home. During your first two weeks you work from the office every day, so you can meet the team.

## Core hours
Core hours are 11:00 to 16:00 Tbilisi time. Be reachable on chat during core hours, wherever you work.

## Working from abroad
You may work from another country for up to 20 working days per year. Ask your team lead and HR in writing at least 2 weeks before you travel.

## Home office equipment
After your probation period, you can claim one-off support of 300 GEL for a chair or a desk for your home office, through the HR portal under Expenses.
