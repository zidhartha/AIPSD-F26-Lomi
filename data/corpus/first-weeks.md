# Your first weeks

## First day
On your first day IT gives you a laptop, and your buddy shows you the office. Set up the password manager and two-factor authentication before lunch. Your team lead books a 30-minute welcome call for the afternoon.

## Your buddy
Every new employee gets a buddy from another team for the first 3 months. Ask your buddy anything, including questions you think are too small for your team lead.

## Probation
Probation lasts 3 months. At the end of the second month your team lead holds a check-in. At the end of the third month you receive written confirmation that probation is complete.

## First code change
In your first week you open one small pull request, reviewed by your buddy. Every pull request needs one approval and green CI before merging.
