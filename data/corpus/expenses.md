# Expenses and reimbursement

## What can be reimbursed
Brightpath reimburses work travel, client meals approved in advance by your team lead, and training courses approved by HR. Personal meals during a normal working day are not reimbursed.

## How to claim an expense
Submit the claim in the HR portal under Expenses within 30 days of the purchase. Attach a photo of the receipt for every item. Claims are paid with the next monthly salary.

## Limits
A single client meal is reimbursed up to 60 GEL per person. A taxi to or from the airport is reimbursed up to 50 GEL per trip. Anything above a limit needs written approval from the finance lead before the purchase.

## Learning budget
Every employee has a learning budget of 1,200 GEL per year for books, courses and conferences. Unused budget does not carry over to the next year.
