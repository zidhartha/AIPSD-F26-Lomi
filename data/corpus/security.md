# Security basics

## Passwords and accounts
Use the company password manager for every work account. Turn on two-factor authentication for email, the code host and the HR portal on your first day. Never share a password, even with IT.

## Laptops
Lock your laptop whenever you leave it. Disk encryption must stay on. Report a lost or stolen laptop to IT within 1 hour, by phone, at any time of day.

## Customer data
Customer data stays in the production systems. Never copy customer data to your laptop, a personal account or a chat. Use the anonymised staging database for testing.

## Reporting an incident
If you think an account was compromised or you clicked a suspicious link, write to the security channel immediately. Do not try to fix it alone. Reporting quickly is never punished.
